      How the Cube works

      The Cube has six faces of nine squares. Each face has its own rule, written above the cube when that face is toward you.

      Every column and row is a ring that wraps all the way around the cube, like a slice of a puzzle cube. Its rule is written beside the face toward you, and it carries on to every face the ring passes over.

      So each square needs a Pokémon that fits three rules: its face, its column and its row. Each Pokémon goes on the cube only once.

      Drag the cube or use the arrows to turn it. Tap a square on the face toward you to answer it; tap a square on another face to turn that face toward you.

      Reverse flips it: all 54 Pokémon are shown and the 15 rules are hidden. Tap a face name or a ring name to guess what its Pokémon share.

      You get 3 lives. Today's Cube is the same for everyone and resets at midnight. Practice Cubes are endless.
