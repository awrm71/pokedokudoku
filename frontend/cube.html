    How the Cube works

    The Cube is three boards stacked on top of each other. Every square sits under a column rule, beside a row rule, and on a layer with its own rule. The Pokémon you put there has to fit all three.

    Open a layer with the tabs, by tapping it in the 3D view, or by swiping across the board. Each Pokémon can only be used once in the whole Cube.

    Reverse flips it: all 27 Pokémon are shown and the nine rules are hidden. Tap a column, row or layer name to guess what its nine Pokémon have in common.

    You get 3 lives. Today's Cube is the same for everyone and resets at midnight. Practice Cubes are endless.
