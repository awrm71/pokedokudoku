Privacy

PokeDokuDoku · last updated October 2026

Playing without an account

Your browser gets a random ID so the game can remember your Pokédex, your Daily board and your stats. It isn't linked to who you are.

Signing in with Google

If you sign in, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We use them only to run your account: to recognise you on any device, show your picture and trainer name, and keep your progress. We never see your Google password, and we can't post anything or read anything else in your Google account.

What we store

  • Your account: name, email, profile picture link and the trainer name you choose.
  • Your game progress: Pokédex entries, shinies, Daily and Reverse Board answers, streaks.
  • A sign-in session cookie so you stay signed in.

Your answers count anonymously toward the percentages everyone sees on the Daily boards. Your trainer name may appear on leaderboards once they launch.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data or share your email with anyone.

Deleting your data

Email us at YOUR-CONTACT-EMAIL and we'll delete your account and everything attached to it.
