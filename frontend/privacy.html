Privacy

Route 81 · last updated October 2026

Playing without an account

Your browser gets a random ID so the game can remember your Pokédex, your Daily board and your stats. It isn't linked to who you are.

Accounts

You can make an account with any email address and a password, or sign in with Google.

  • Email and password: we store your email and a scrambled (hashed) copy of your password. We can't read your password. If you ask to reset it, we email you a one-time code.
  • Google: Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We never see your Google password, and we can't post anything or read anything else in your Google account.

Either way, we use these only to run your account: to recognise you on any device and keep your progress.

What we store

  • Your account: email, the trainer name you choose, your partner Pokémon, and your Google picture link if you use Google.
  • Your game progress: Pokédex entries, shinies, Daily and Reverse Board answers, streaks, and whether you used a revive.
  • A sign-in cookie so you stay signed in.

Your answers count anonymously toward the percentages everyone sees on the Daily boards. Your trainer name is public and may appear on leaderboards once they launch. Your email is never shown.

Ads

The site is free and paid for by ads from Google. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads, and may use them to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads in Google's Ad Settings, and read how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first.

A revive ad is only shown when you ask for one.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data or share your email with anyone.

Deleting your data

Email us at YOUR-CONTACT-EMAIL and we'll delete your account and everything attached to it.
