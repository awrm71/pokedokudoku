Route 81 · last updated November 2026

These are the ground rules for using Route 81 (route81.app). By playing, you agree to them. They're written to be read, not to trip you up.

The game

Route 81 is a free fan-made puzzle game. It's provided as is: we work to keep it running and correct, but we can't promise it will always be available, error-free, or that every category ruling will match your own reading of the games. If you think an answer was judged wrongly, email us and we'll look.

Accounts

  • One account per person, please. Keep your sign-in details to yourself; you're responsible for what happens on your account.
  • Your trainer name is public (it can appear on leaderboards), so choose one that isn't offensive, hateful, or pretending to be someone else. We may rename or remove accounts that break this.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and everything attached to it at any time. See Privacy.

Play fair

Don't use bots, scripts or automated tools to play, scrape answers, or interfere with the site, and don't try to manipulate stats, streaks or leaderboards. Looking things up in a Pokédex while you play is completely fine; that's half the fun.

Route 81 is supported by ads served by Google. Please don't click ads you aren't interested in, or ask others to; it can get the site's ad account suspended. How ads use cookies is explained in Privacy.
